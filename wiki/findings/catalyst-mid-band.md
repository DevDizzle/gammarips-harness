# Catalyst mid-band (score 0.5–0.8) is the only replicated positive pocket

In the 2026-07-17 lever-court study (n=1,806 labeled 3d rows), catalyst_score bands were
the pre-registered H1. Result — NOT the expected monotone "stronger catalyst is better":
- **Mid band 0.5–0.8 (n=467): the only cohort positive and >45% win in BOTH split
  halves** — full +4.0% avg / 47% win; halves +8.7%/51% and +1.4%/45%; touch +40% 37%.
- High band ≥0.8 (n=986): negative in both halves (−1.9% / −5.8%; win 40/39). The most
  headline-heavy names underperform — consistent with crowded/priced-in news.
- Low band <0.5 (n=341): flips (+9.7% on n=35 → −5.4% on n=306). No claim.

Why fragile, stated plainly: the pre-registered direction was "high beats low" and the
data instead surfaced mid-beats-both — a pattern found among ~20 examined cells, so
multiple-comparisons risk is real even with split-half replication. Effect size is
modest (win 47% vs pool 42%). Labels are the legacy fixed bracket, not our trail exit.
catalyst_score is the enrichment's own composite, not a pure earnings-expectation
measure ([[earnings-iv-crush]] still governs holding through prints; the thesis behind
this study was post-catalyst drift, see journal 2026-07-17).

Application: a **tilt, never a gate** — prefer mid-band catalyst names on the shortlist;
a high-band or low-band name needs another reason but is not excluded. Re-score after
~2 weeks of new labels; promote or kill on replication. Related: [[moneyness-10-15-otm]]
(falsified same session), [[mom-60-conditional-lever]] (untested this study — the field
is absent from outcome rows, a logged product gap).

**Exploratory refinement 2026-07-17 evening (POST-HOC — one credibility rung below the
main finding; trader's priced-in-the-day-before mechanism, PYPL 07-15→17 as the
motivating case):** conditioning the mid band on a not-yet-extended tape improves and
stabilizes it — `cat 0.5–0.8 & rsi_14 < 65`: n=395, win 47/46% and avg **+5.3% / +1.9%
in both halves**. The "loud and chased" cell (`cat ≥ 0.8 & rsi ≥ 70`) flips (+2.3 →
−9.1) — directionally supportive of the day-late story but not stable enough to claim.
Surprise worth keeping: `rsi < 50` is STABLY NEGATIVE (−5.7/−5.2) — beaten-down names
in this bullish-flow pool do not bounce; "not extended" ≠ "cheap entry." Docketed for
the 2-week re-score alongside the main band; until then the RSI condition is select-time
color, not a lever.
